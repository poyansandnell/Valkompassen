---
name: Neutrality wording policy
description: How to phrase "oberoende"/neutrality claims in Valkompass copy
---

The product may be described as "oberoende" (not owned/controlled by any party, Valmyndigheten, or media group), but copy must NOT claim that the people behind it are politically inactive or belong to no party.

**Why:** The owner is politically active (stated July 2026). Claims like "vi tillhör inga politiska partier" about the team are false; product-level independence claims are fine.

**How to apply:** In all app/video/marketing copy, phrase neutrality about the service ("tjänsten ägs inte av något parti", "alla partier behandlas lika"), never about the people ("vi tillhör inga partier").
